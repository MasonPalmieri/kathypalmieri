About Kathy

Kathy Palmieri is a retired 3rd grade teacher with a lifelong passion for helping children succeed. She spent the majority of her distinguished career teaching in Woonsocket, Rhode Island.

Throughout her career, Kathy worked extensively with students who have learning disabilities and Individualized Education Plans (IEPs), providing personalized support to help every child thrive.

She now offers private, in-home supplementary tutoring services for elementary students throughout Northern Rhode Island.
