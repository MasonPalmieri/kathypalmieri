About Kathy

Kathy Palmieri is a retired 3rd grade teacher who spent the majority of her distinguished career teaching in Woonsocket, Rhode Island.

She worked extensively with students with learning disabilities and Individualized Education Plans (IEPs), providing individualized, compassionate instruction tailored to each child.

She now offers private, in-home supplementary tutoring services for elementary students throughout Northern Rhode Island.
