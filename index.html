About

Meet Kathy

Kathy Palmieri is a retired third-grade teacher who spent the majority of her distinguished career in Woonsocket, Rhode Island. With more than three decades in the classroom, she developed deep expertise in helping young learners find their footing — especially those who needed a little extra support.

Throughout her career, Kathy worked extensively with students with learning disabilities and Individualized Education Plans (IEPs), providing compassionate, tailored instruction that honored each child's unique way of learning.

Now she brings that same warmth, patience, and expertise to private tutoring — giving every child the one-on-one attention they deserve, right in the comfort of their own home.
